### About Me

Senior Software Engineer with **6+ years of experience** specializing in architecting high-performance mobile and web applications, backend cloud infrastructure, and integrating **IoT devices over Bluetooth (BLE) and Wi-Fi** across diverse domains including high-scale food ordering (IRCTC eCatering), corporate wellness, mobility, and fintech KYC.

- **Current Role**: Senior Software Engineer at **Ipsator** (Bengaluru, Karnataka)
- **High-Scale Systems**: Leading development on **Food On Track (IRCTC eCatering 5M+ downloads)**, IRCTC Lakshadweep Ticketing, and IRCTC Partner Vendor apps.
- **Key Achievements**:
  - Boosted Play Store rating from **3.5 to 4.8** on Food On Track via UX overhauls & native in-app review flows.
  - Increased daily sales by **30%** and DAU by **20%** with smart recommendation systems and dynamic discounts.
  - Developed & deployed cross-platform apps using **Kotlin Multiplatform (KMP)** and iOS WebKit.
- **Specialties**: Android SDK, Jetpack Compose, Kotlin Multiplatform (KMP), Node.js, IoT Device Integration (Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, ESP32), Room Database, Paging 3, Clean Architecture.
